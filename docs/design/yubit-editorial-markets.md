# Sculpted Signals

For the four automated 1200×675 Telegram/Discord posters, the checked-in `yubit-market-signals-preview.png`, `yubit-weekly-calendar-preview.png`, and `yubit-data-update-preview.png` files are the visual source of truth. Preserve their warm-paper field, carbon-black geometry, muted measurement lines, mustard-gold accent, narrow uppercase eyebrow, and template-specific information systems. The public output is publisher-neutral: do not render YUBIT/YUBIT Academy, a logo, mascot, QR code, or official-author claim.

Do not reinterpret the template as a blue/white dashboard, dark card system, rounded-pill interface, neon crypto graphic, or token-badge layout. Adapt only the verified dynamic content, information density, and the matching fourth-product composition. Weekly Catalysts is one Monday–Sunday UTC week and renders only populated verified events.

Sculpted Signals treats information as an object moving through space. Monumental black forms establish structure, while market curves, capsules, and nodes cross their boundaries to reveal momentum and interruption. Empty space is active: it separates decisions, creates pace, and lets a small number of facts carry unusual visual weight. Every alignment must feel meticulously crafted rather than merely placed.

Warm white behaves like editorial paper, carbon black provides architectural gravity, and cool grey creates quiet measurement. Mustard amber appears only at genuine points of urgency—never as decoration. Colour therefore becomes a disciplined signal system, calibrated with painstaking attention so importance can be understood before the words are read.

Scale creates the rhythm. One oversized date, indicator, or directional glyph anchors the canvas; compact numerical modules orbit it with generous margins. Repetition is sparse and exact. The composition should feel asymmetrical but balanced, the product of countless refinements by a designer at the top of the field.

Typography is clinical and minimal: small uppercase labels, decisive numerals, and brief interpretive phrases. Paragraphs do not belong inside the image. Data relationships are expressed through position, contrast, rails, and crossings; text only names what the spatial system already communicates. Craft is visible in optical spacing, not ornamental density.

The quiet conceptual reference is market information passing through a frame: price and policy do not sit neatly inside boxes, but bend, interrupt, and reconfigure them. That motion should be felt rather than illustrated literally. The final work must appear laboured over with care, original in construction, and precise enough to function as both a decision instrument and a publishing artifact.
